# Commands: every backtest mode and every model.py command

What each `backtest.py` mode replays, which constants it fits and how long it takes, and `model.py`'s JSON commands with their flags. Moved here from CLAUDE.md on Nov 15, 2026, word for word. **Find the mode that covers a constant before changing it, and rerun it after.**

Python lives in `.venv` (Homebrew Python blocks global pip): use `.venv/bin/python` or `source .venv/bin/activate`.
- `python test_model.py`: self-check, synthetic data, no network. Run it after touching `model.py`.
- Every `model.py` command's bad input (the model's `KeyError`: a player not found, a date or week out of range, a move that can't be made) prints one line, `{"error": "..."}`, and exits 1, as the app's routes return (S4, Nov 19); before, a Python traceback.
- `python backtest.py`: calibration check (~1 min). Run it after changing projections or odds maths. `python backtest.py replay`: rest-of-season error by expert weight (fits `PROJ_W`). `python backtest.py experts`: Yahoo's line vs ESPN's as the expert, head to head on the text snapshots (same day, 7 and 14 days old, ESPN first with Yahoo filling in), for everyone and for players out on the snapshot; ~3 s (B22). `python backtest.py stream [base] [pos] [opp] [held] [beam] [adds]`: streaming policies on the same simulated team-weeks with real box scores, 2022-23 to 2025-26, 5 leagues (seasons and leagues in parallel; first checks `stream_delta()`, a swap's lineup change, against the lineup recomputed, `stream_check()`). `base` (the default, ~2 min): most games, generic value, this week's categories (from Monday's projection or the real totals so far), waiting till Wed / Thu / Fri, games then categories, only the categories you're behind in, and timed (`planner`, the app's This week). Categories and matchups won against a team that never streams, paired (se clustered by week); adds by weekday; by Monday's projected margin; the week as played on Monday's model (`expected`). `pos`: the real league's slots with Yahoo positions (`positions()`: the snapshots' players, the rest guessed from their 5 nearest by per-36 stats; ~2 min). `opp`: the opponent streams too, by games then by the planner (~8 min). `held`: mid-week news (a player who sat a game his team played is out from the next day, until a guessed return) and the last add kept for it until Fri / Sun / news only (M17's late-news add; ~3 min). `beam`: the whole week's plan searched each morning: greedy on top of the moves before like `_plan()` (`seq`, `seq-all` with every free agent), from the 8 best first moves like `this_week()`'s paths (`seq8`), a beam of 8 (~5 min). `adds`: the planner with 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 adds, and dropping only players with no games left (~4 min). Policies and options: `POLICY`, `STREAM_RUNS` (Nov 22, checked Nov 23: ROADMAP M32). `python backtest.py habits` (Y5, Nov 24, ~25 s): each team's habits on Yahoo's daily lineups (`data/yahoo_lineups.json`, `python yahoo.py lineups`) and the box scores: starting slot-days empty / with no game / sat, starts lost a day, banked / best lineup (`model.habits()`'s rate), how often the per-game guess equals the real starters, adds a week and the share streamed; then every pre-tip snapshot of a finished week, each team's rest of the week against what it really banked: shipped vs the habit (each week left out of the fit, and as replayed: the days before the snapshot) vs a streaming term, z², PTS error, category and matchup log loss with se (fits `HABIT_K`; rerun as weeks pile up). `python backtest.py fill`: the real league's missed games and the share covered by an IL move or a full lineup, overall, by length of absence and by team (checks `FILL`; seconds; rerun as snapshots pile up). `python backtest.py teammates`: next week's error with teammates' injuries (fits `SHARE` / `USAGE`, ~3 min). `python backtest.py onoff [season]`: the same by `ONOFF_K` (each team's own split, ~5 min). `python backtest.py rates [season]`: the same split for production per minute by stat group (M22, Nov 3: no better on 2022-23 to 2025-26, not shipped; FG% splits don't repeat, ~4 min). `python backtest.py minor [season]`: the same with 15–25 min sitters handing out `SHARE_LOW` of their minutes (`REGULAR` 15) and the `CAP_GIVE` cap (M25, Nov 4: stats worse in 2025-26 and 2024-25, cap no change; both off, ~25 min). `python backtest.py backups [season]` (add `usage` for minutes only): 15–25 min sitters' minutes to the like-for-like backup (`ROLE_LOW`, `USAGE_LOW`) or only where each team's split shows it (Nov 17, AUDIT.md: backups' minutes better, stats worse in all four seasons 2022-23 to 2025-26; off, ~15 min a season). `python backtest.py bench`: rest-of-season error by `BENCH_MIN` for players no expert projects, 2022-23 to 2025-26 (M26, ~10 min). `python backtest.py rookies`: the same by a rookie's expert weight and rate shrink (fits `ROOKIE_W` / `ROOKIE_K`, audit #3, ~1 min). `python backtest.py yahoo`: this season's snapshots, each one's Yahoo lines vs each player's first-seen one, against the games after it (audit #2, Nov 4: the live line stays; rerun as snapshots pile up, seconds). `python backtest.py ahead`: from every day of the week, each game left for players who played their team's last game vs `fresh_plays()`, back-to-backs apart (M27, M28: fits `B2B`; ~1 min). `python backtest.py gameday`: how often listed players played (fits `GAMEDAY`, seconds). `python backtest.py trend` (M8): a 14-day window's gap from rest-of-season value by the games it holds, and whether the next window keeps it (fits `TREND`, seconds). `python backtest.py returns` (B11): the short ramp on the games back after an absence (`RAMP`) and how often a player plays his j-th game from ESPN's return date (`RETURN_LATE`). `python backtest.py opponent` (D1, Nov 24, ~15 s): every game next week of a 15+ minute line on each Monday's `ros()`, 2022-23 to 2025-26, actual / line by fifth of the NBA opponent's factor with and without it, the squared error a game it saves by season, then each season's weekly replay on the real schedule with `OPP_K` off and on (checks `OPP_K`; not `stream opp`, that's the streaming opponent). `python backtest.py corr`: how a week's category misses move together and matchup log loss / calibration by `WEEK_LOAD`, 2022-23 to 2025-26, both modes (M9, ~30 s). `python backtest.py league`: the real league end to end, each pre-tip snapshot's rest-of-week totals and category odds vs what the teams banked by the week's last snapshot (Nov 9, weeks 2–3: totals within 2% but FTM +5%, BLK −5%, TO +6%; misses 0.8–1.3 of the model's sd; log loss 0.377 vs 0.415 if calibrated; ~40 s, rerun as snapshots pile up; built on `model.replay()` since Q33, the same leagues as `data/history.jsonl`, and it ends with each finished week's call on all six matchups and every category against how the week ended (Yahoo's final score where a snapshot lists it), the count League's Season so far shows, and says whether `data/history.jsonl` gives the same). `python backtest.py lineups`: whose games count on a night the snapshot was pulled before tip-off, the per-game guess vs the snapshot's lineup against the next snapshot's totals, and This week's expected starts for that night (B8, ~25 s; rerun as snapshots pile up). `python backtest.py news`: how often a player who's playing is newly ruled out mid-week, and for how long (fits `HAZARD` / `ONE_GAME`, seconds). `python backtest.py availability`: each Monday's games-played guess vs the real share, 2022-23 to 2025-26 (fits `AVAIL_*`, and `STATUS_AVAIL` from players who sat their team's last game). `python backtest.py playoffs`: each Monday's playoff and title odds vs what happened, 60 leagues, average weeks then the real schedule (M2) (fits `SEASON_SD` / `WEEK_SD`, ~10 min; rerun Nov 24 after M30's `season_sim(meet=)`, which draws nothing new: the same numbers). `python backtest.py recency`: rest-of-season error by minutes shrink and recent games' weight on 2022-23 to 2025-26 (fits `MIN_SHRINK` / `RECENT`, seconds). `python backtest.py season`: the season line's rest-of-season error by `MIN_SHRINK_SEASON` and `NET_SEASON`, known lifts on, 2022-23 to 2025-26 (A6/A7, ~1 min).
- `python yahoo.py`: pull the league from Yahoo's API into `data/league.json` and `snapshots/yahoo-<day>.json` (Y2, Nov 24: ~1 s and 10–11 calls with the hour's rank pages in `data/yahoo_ranks.json`, ~2 s and 31 calls without; it prints both); `python yahoo.py lineups`: every team's lineup on each past day and the season's add / drop log into `data/yahoo_lineups.json` (Y5: only days not in it yet, one call a day; nothing when it has yesterday); `python yahoo.py check`: a pull next to the newest text snapshot (rank pages always fresh), section by section, with last week's final scores against the snapshot's, writing only the raw XML to `data/yahoo_raw/` (one file per path) (run it after changing a field path in `pull()`); `python yahoo.py import <file>.txt ["Team"]`: a text snapshot instead; `python yahoo.py demo`: a fake league.
- `python app.py --no-browser`: run the app without opening a tab. `#view`, `?punt=FT%25,TOV` and `?aim=title` deep-link a view, a build and the aim (M30).
- `python model.py {team|trade|ideas|search|waivers|movers|roster|week|strategy|news|compare|schedule|move|sources|check|drop|watch|today|recap|playoffs} [--punt ...] [--aim season|title]`: prints JSON (M30, Nov 24: `playoffs` = League's Playoffs period, `playoff_view()`: each playoff round's reach, your games, the likely opponents with your odds by category and where you're thin, plus `dates` (key dates with days to go, `dates()`), ~0.1 s, ~7 KB; `--aim title` on `strategy` (plans, ranking and pick on title odds, `season_pick`), `trade` and `move` (the verdict on title odds, `season_verdict` beside it), `waivers` (each suggested move's `title`, most title first) and `search` (its goal defaults to `title`); `waivers --cats C` / `week --cats C` = Play for (Q32): Waivers ranked on those categories (`goal_fit`), This week's streams and plan on them; `watch [--player X | --remove X]` = the watchlist, `data/watch.json`, Q32; `ideas --mode fair|finesse`; `search` also takes `--give`, `--get`, `--lock`, `--tier`, `--size`, `--healthy`, `--shape`, `--max-overpay` and `--outside`; `trade` / `search` take `--no-free` and `--their-free`, and `--healthy-fa` works on any command; `compare --players "A, B"`, Q3; `schedule` = the schedule planner, `League.planner()`, Q4; `move --add A --drop B [--day D]` = the add/drop evaluator, `League.add_drop()`, Q7; `sources` = when each cache was fetched, `freshness()`, Q9; `wants --team T` = what T wants from you, `League.wants()`, Q17; `lineup [--vs T] [--cats C]` = this week's start/sit for the most category wins (or just those categories), `League.start_sit()`, Q18; `search --goal title` = the finder ranked on your title odds, Q24, searching on the playoff weeks since M30 (`po_score`, ~10 s on the real league, ~11 KB at the CLI's 20); `note [--text T]` = write a note for Claude Code to `data/notes.md`, or list the open ones, no league loaded; `ahead --week N [--weeks M] [--add A] [--drop B]` = a later schedule week or several, `League.weeks_ahead()`, also `week --week N` / `move --week N`; `what [--player X --line/--pct/--min/--health/--until | --clear]` = set, clear or list what-ifs, `whatif()`; `what --plan --add A --drop B --activate C --park D` = record a plan pick (picked, not made on Yahoo yet; without `--plan` it's an error since Y3: moves made on Yahoo come with the pull), `what --undo ID|all`, `League.made()` / `moves()`, Q30: every later command is on it and starts with `roster_moves`; `what` alone prints `{players, moves}`; `check` = what's wrong with your roster now (a healthy player stuck in an IL slot, too many players, an open spot, an injured one who could park, today's lineup; `notes`: a player due back from an IL slot this week, left parked or activated on which day, B17), `League.roster_check()`, Q29; `drop [--vs T]` = Who to drop: every cut's this week, next week, season, playoff and title odds, who goes next when an IL player is back, and the picks, `League.drops()`, ~1 s, Q29; `move --drop X` with no `--add` = a drop alone, which activates a player stuck in an IL slot). `strategy` (the punt planner) takes ~45 s, about the same with `--aim title`. `note --close N --text "why"` closes open note N (1 = the first `note` lists): ticked with the reason, not deleted. `lineup` prints `week`'s `lineups` (on the plan's roster, Nov 17; takes `--keep`, `--weight`, `--cats`, `--choose`). `week` / `lineup` / `today --skip "A, B"` = free agents to leave out (taken, or not wanted), `League.without()`, Nov 19. `week --choose KEY` = a path from Every path (its `key`, "none" = no move) as the plan (Use this). `week` / `lineup --adds N` = the best path with N adds as the plan (0 = no move; more than there are: the most there are), B20, Nov 23. `week --pool "A, B"` = the only players streams may drop (B21; `picked`, nothing charged); `week` prints `drop_cost` (what each of `drops` costs the season, B21) and `claims` (your pending Yahoo claims, already counted; Q35) and `claim` / `submit_by` on a plan move or stream that's a claim to put in before a player back from an IL slot blocks adds. `week` / `lineup --deep` = the deep search (`League._deep()`: a beam of 1000 whole plans over every healthy free agent on every open day, from each way to activate a player back from an IL slot; ~15–20 s on the real league, `deep` in the output = its seconds; the quick plan ~2 s); its paths and plan can carry `activate`, and their keys end "activate DAY A>CUT" / "park A". `recap [--week N]` = last week (or week N) against its call and the season so far, `League.recap()`, Q33: `history()` first brings `data/history.jsonl` up to date (each new snapshot replayed, ~1 s; the first run backfills all, ~25 s), ~10 KB; `today [--vs T] [--keep "A, B"] [--weight ...]` = game day: tonight's games with tip times and the lock, both lineups with live box-score lines, the to-do before tip, tonight's totals vs expected, the week day by day, `League.game_night()`, ~2 s, ~4 KB (Q31). `week` / `lineup` take `--weight "PTS=2,TOV=0.5"` (Q34: lean on categories, 0 to 5, the rest count 1; the picks lean, the odds printed stay real).
