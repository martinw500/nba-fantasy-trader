## 1. The matchup model and its calibration

- 9-cat advice (trade, ideas, waivers, standings) runs on the matchup model: `week` (per-game line × GAMES_WEEK × avail, missed games half-filled at waiver level) → `cat_probs` → `_score` → `outlook()`. `_score` is the share of categories won (`settings.scoring` "categories", the default and Martin's league) or `match_prob` ("one_win"). This week's chance to win the matchup (M9, Nov 5) has the categories move together: `match_prob(p, cats, WEEK_LOAD)` loads each category's margin 0.5 on one shared factor (counting stats +, TO −, FG% / FT% 0; probit, `phinv()`, 5 Gauss-Hermite nodes `GH`), so each category's own odds stay and a categories league's numbers don't move; only `this_week()`'s `win`, `add_drop()`'s week `win` and, in One Win, `_wscore()` (streams, the add plan, `_reserve()`, Start / sit, Try a move's week) use it. Season-long `_score` keeps the categories independent (on an average week the factor measured better in 1 of 4 seasons, bins worse in 3, and a One Win search would run ~9 → 35 s). `python backtest.py corr` fits it (real schedule, matchup log loss better every season, also with the load fitted on the other three); `report()` prints both. `season_sim()`'s `WEEK_SD` is a related shift (every category the same way, TO included, fitted on playoff odds), left separate. Deltas are in win-% points, `unit: "win%"`. Win-% cutoffs (`TIERS`, `VERDICT`, `MOVE`, `GUARD`, `accept()`) are in One Win terms, scaled by `League.s` (`CAT_SCALE` 0.4 in categories). z-scores (`value()`) are only for player tables and candidate selection.
- DISPERSION, SCHED_CV and PCT_NOISE are fitted by `python backtest.py` (2025-26 box scores, 20 simulated leagues, predictions from what was known each Monday, regulars who sat their team's last game out until a noisy return, `RETURN_NOISE`). Re-run it after changing projections (`baseline()`, `prior()`, `ros()`, availability) and keep the calibration tables near the diagonal. GAMES_WEEK is still a guess (STATUS_AVAIL's SUSP too). FILL (0.5) was one until Nov 16: `python backtest.py fill` counts every game a rostered player missed on a snapshot's day as his team stood that morning (parked in an IL slot with the active spots full, so his replacement plays; in an active slot on a night 10 active teammates played; or not covered; players who hadn't played yet this season left out, since `vec()` counts a parked player exactly): **0.47 of 245 missed games covered** (0.36 by an IL move, 0.11 lineup full anyway), 0.20 of first missed games and 0.76 from the 6th on, so 0.5 stays. It is a manager's habit more than a constant: 0.11 (Mathmagicians) to 0.93 (fatty zhou, 14 of 15; Martin: "if he gets injured i can js ir him and pick pj up after"). One number for every team is the known miss (ROADMAP M31, not built: at 0.9 for fatty zhou alone his season win % reads 59.1 → 64.9 on a 15-game sample, while Washington for Suggs only goes −0.21 → −0.36 and Suggs stays among his three cheapest). After any change, check standings stay realistic: about 36–59% on the real league as categories since A5 (Nov 7: Mathmagicians 35.8%, 35.3% before A6/A7; 37–59% after N9's availability, 41–57% after N5's IL slots; 37–63% before them; 19–80% as One Win back then). The snake-drafted demo is tighter (~47–52%).
- Category odds are calibrated, so cutoffs live in odds terms: `WEAK` (35%) = weak spot / punt hint, and This week's Ahead/Behind calls use the same cutoff.
- Punts concede the category (`_concede` sets your odds to 0) for your side only. Opponents always use real odds. This week doesn't concede (B10, Nov 6, Martin: Yahoo scores the matchup on every category): `_matchup()` and `_week_plan()` are real odds, so its win, score, streams, plan, Start / sit and Try a move's week; the punt still picks who's cut for a player back (`_week_roster()` → `_il_cut(punt)`) and every season number.
- `build()` = a team's weak spots (categories it wins <35% of weeks, `WEAK`). Points-mode trades value opponents on it.

## 2. Projections and player values

- Values are per game, rest of season: `ros()` = minutes (`GP/(GP+MIN_SHRINK)`, 3: fast, roles change) × production per minute (this season's minutes played vs `SHRINK` games of baseline minutes, slow; since A5, Nov 6, each stat at its own pace: `RATE_K` FGA 5, AST 15, STL 60, the rest `SHRINK`, all × k / `SHRINK` for rookies and `HYPE`; FG% / FT% at `PCT_K` 40 / 60 games of baseline attempts, makes = attempts × %, PTS = 2 FGM + 3PM + FTM; shooters 5+ points off their prior after 3–4 weeks keep 1–25% of the gap, the one k assumed 36–40%), on this season's line from `recent()`: box scores with each game weighted 0.5^(days ago / `RECENT` 45), GP the real count (fetch()'s line without box scores, or where their games don't equal fetch()'s GP; `gamelogs()` skips games still being played). Regulars and lifts read that same line. `had()` weighs the lifts players had the same way. `SHRINK` was picked replaying 2025-26 (Oct 27); `MIN_SHRINK` and `RECENT` on 2022-23 to 2025-26 (`python backtest.py recency`, Oct 30; rerun Nov 4 after M26 and A2: vs the old line weeks 1–6 +1.3 / −0.8 / +0.1 / −0.2%, season −0.4 / −1.4 / −0.3 / −1.3%; `MIN_SHRINK` 4 edged 3 on the season by 0.04–0.32% but the weekly matchup backtest, `python backtest.py [season]`, was worse at 4 and 6 in three of four seasons, so both stay). That is This week's line (`p_week`). Two lines since A6/A7 (Nov 7): the season line `p` (trades, standings, Waivers' season gains, Strategy, playoff weeks after this one) is `ros()` at `MIN_SHRINK_SEASON` (6) on this season less `NET_SEASON` (0.5) of `had()` (`net()`), plus `ahead`; `p_week` is `MIN_SHRINK` on all of it netted, plus this week's lifts (`python backtest.py season`, 2022-23 to 2025-26: rest-of-season error −1.87 / −0.28 / −3.09 / −2.04% vs the old season line; no netting lost 2023-24, +0.68%; a minutes-only half-life another −0.1%, not shipped; playoff odds 0.3306 either way, title 0.2771 → 0.2750; This week, `teammates` and the weekly backtest unchanged). z-scores, `week`, `vec()` and `_sched_week()` read `p`; daily lineup order (`_value`) reads it too (no This week number moved, Nov 7). backtest.py's `projections()`, `replay()` and `mondays()` read `recent()` too, and `season_parts()` / `season_line()` build `p` for `season` and `playoffs`: keep them the same maths. `ros()` runs on `prior()`: `baseline()` blended with an expert line at `PROJ_W`, then a veteran's production per minute × (1 + `AGE_SLOPE` −0.0075 × (age − 27), ages 20–36; `baseline()`'s AGE = last season's + 1 from `fetch()`'s AGE column, a cache without it is refetched; offline it's off) (0.6, fitted by `python backtest.py replay` on ESPN's 2025-26 preseason projections). A rookie (no baseline; `prior()` marks him GP 0) blends at `ROOKIE_W` (0.75) and his production per minute leans on this season at `ROOKIE_K` (10) games, not `SHRINK`, rookies no expert projects too (audit #3, Nov 4, `python backtest.py rookies`: projected rookies' rest-of-season error −1.5 / −5.5 / −2.5 / −0.5% on 2022-23 to 2025-26, everyone's −0.07 to −0.28%; the expert line alone was worse in 2 of 4). An expert row with no numbers at all is no row, not a zero line (ESPN's 2023-24 file had 43, mostly that season's rookies). The expert line (`experts()`) is Yahoo's from the snapshot where it has the player (the live one: Yahoo updates it in-season, and freezing it at the first snapshot that lists him measured worse on this season's snapshots, `python backtest.py yahoo`, audit #2, Nov 4), else ESPN's (also when Yahoo's PTS+REB+AST is under half of ESPN's: some Yahoo rows have missed games baked in, Hayes 1.7 pts at 27 min; with no ESPN row, under half of the baseline's or this season's with 3+ games drops it to no expert line at all, Nov 4 audit #1: Kobe Sanders 1.9 pts, 11.0 this season). Yahoo has no minutes: they're ESPN's or ours scaled by output. A rookie's baseline is the fringe line. A player no expert projects (M26, Nov 4) has the fringe line's part of his baseline (`baseline()`'s `PULL` / `PULL_MIN`) at `BENCH_MIN` (12) minutes, his rates kept (`prior()`), and an unprojected rookie in `ros()` starts on the fringe line's rates at `BENCH_MIN` minutes (`python backtest.py bench`: −2.1% rest-of-season error over 2022-23 to 2025-26, every season; −2.2% rerun after A2, 15 tying it; hold its leagues' rosters fixed when comparing calibration, since the draft reads the projections (A5, Nov 6: a plain `python backtest.py` drafts from the new prior, and its age term made the simulated leagues more even, so its log loss rose though every season's predictions got better on the same rosters)). ## 4. Teammates' injuries

- Teammates' injuries (`lifts()`): regulars are players in `League.here` (a game this season or an ESPN listing; before any games, everyone): one who's neither has left the league, not sat out (`had()` took up to 9 min a game off his old teammates for Westbrook and co.), and the market ranks him last. While a regular (`REGULAR`+ min) with an ESPN return date sits, `SHARE` (0.5) of his minutes go to teammates in the rotation by room under `ROOM` (a cap: the rest goes to the bench) and role similarity, plus `USAGE` of the shots, points, assists and turnovers those minutes don't replace. Everyone out at once sits together (M4): a team's players out with a return date form stretches between their returns (`stretches()`), and in each one nobody out takes minutes and the sitters share the room, most minutes first. Each team's own split (M13, `onoff()`, `League.adj`): per receiver, a ridge of his minutes this season on which of its regulars sat (`sat()`, `recent()`'s weights), each sitter's effect pulled toward the rule's minutes as if they were `ONOFF_K` (4) more games, moves the rule's minutes (within `ROOM`, never below zero); minutes only, shots stay on the rule (Fears: +1.3 → +2.7 min while Murray sits). A sitter out since before the season (no games in it) was in the preseason projection, so `priced_in()` cuts his lift by the expert line's part of the receiver's minutes. `ros()` reads this season net of `had()` (the lifts players had in games regulars sat, those out together counted together), so a boost isn't counted twice: all of it This week, `NET_SEASON` (0.5) of it in the season line (A6: absences recur and availability already counts the starter's missed games, so netting all of it ran season lines low; next week, all of it is right). `_boost()` adds each stretch's lift for each player's games in it: rest of season in `p`, this week in `p_week` (`week_vec()` reads it). Start / sit (`_week_plan`) instead plays each game on its own day's stretch: the line less the averaged lift (`League.ahead`, or `_boost(week_end)` this week) plus that day's `self.lift` rows, so the games sum to the same week. `League.__init__`, `had()` and `backtest.py`'s `lifted()` / `season_parts()` must stay the same maths. Lifts only make sense with the injured player sitting; otherwise his minutes count twice.
