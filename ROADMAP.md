**Everything already built is in `docs/HISTORY.md`** (the roadmap as it stood on Nov 16, word for word, 410 KB, with later done briefs appended at its end). When an item is done: tick it in the pathway, put a dated "Done" note with the numbers measured under its brief, update README / `docs/MODEL.md` / `docs/FILES.md` / `docs/COMMANDS.md` / the gm skill where they describe it, run `python test_model.py`, and restart the app in Martin's Terminal. ## M32. Each player's own volatility in This week's odds `xhigh`

**Nov 24 (Martin, on Bilal Coulibaly: "sometimes good games sometimes stinkers"):** his game-to-game variance / mean this season (15+ minute games, players with 10+): PTS 2.86 against the league median 2.61 (swingier than 61%), REB 0.91 (32%), AST 0.95 (39%), STL 1.30 (74%), BLK 0.83 (22%), 3PM 1.59 (85%). So he's about average except threes and steals, on 18 games, and his stinkers are mostly a low average (0-for-8). Whether any of this repeats is what M32 measures.

**Why (Martin, Nov 17):** "later in the week when winning and losing categories are more consolidated then thats when specialists and some streams actually show themselves ... take into account more things like recent performance, environment so performance boost and volatility of their recent performance and expectation". Specialists are already found (streams rank on this week's odds, every healthy free agent timed since Nov 17; Oct 31: STL the one close category, all five top streams steals players, Thybulle 31 → 50%). Recent form (`recent()`), teammates out (`lift`, by day), chance to play, back-to-backs and ramps are in. What isn't: **a player's own spread**. `cat_probs()` takes each category's variance as one league-wide `DISPERSION` × the mean (plus `SCHED_CV`, `PCT_NOISE` on shooting), so a boom-or-bust 3PM shooter and a steady one with the same average count alike. Behind in a close category you want the swingy one, ahead the steady one; Start / sit and streams can't tell them apart.

**What's there (read `docs/MODEL.md` §1 first):** `cat_probs()` (variance per category from team totals), `DISPERSION` / `PCT_NOISE` fitted by `python backtest.py` on 2025-26 box scores, `week_vec()` (team totals only: the variance would need a per-player sum), `backtest.py`'s replay and calibration tables, `gamelogs_*.csv` for 2022-23 to 2025-26.

**Measure first:**
1. Each player's game-to-game variance / mean per counting stat (and makes-given-attempts spread for FG% / FT%) over one stretch (say weeks 1–8), shrunk toward the league `DISPERSION` by games played; does it predict the same ratio over the next stretch (weeks 9–16)? Correlation and the shrink k that fits best, 2022-23 to 2025-26. A simulation adds nothing over the analytic sum: the question is only whether the per-player numbers repeat.
2. If they do: `cat_probs()` with each side's variance = Σ starts × that player's dispersion × his line, in the weekly matchup backtest (`python backtest.py`, schedule mode). Ship only if log loss improves in 3 of 4 seasons and calibration stays near the diagonal.
3. If they don't: write the numbers here and close it (the league-wide figure is then right, and a "volatility" tag would mislead).
