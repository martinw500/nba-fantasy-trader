# nba-fantasy-trader (Fantasy GM)

Local Yahoo NBA fantasy GM app. Python 3.13 + pandas backend, one-file vanilla HTML/JS UI. User-facing docs and every workflow: README.md. **What's untested and what to build next: ROADMAP.md**, whose **pathway** at the top is the one ordered list of open work. Read it before starting new work and tick items off (in the pathway and in the item's section) as they're done.

## AI GM notes
The AI GM chat can't change code; it writes what Martin flags (wrong numbers, missed rules, feature asks) to `data/notes.md` with `model.py note --text` (`model.note()`, ai.py's `note` tool). **Before new work, read the open ones (`- [ ]`)** and tell Martin what's there. When one is handled, tick it (`- [x]`) and add the date and what changed; if it's not a bug, say why on the line.

## Files
- `model.py`: the engine (`League` class) plus a JSON CLI. All numbers come from here: UI, AI chat and the `/gm` skill.
- `yahoo.py`: league snapshot → `data/league.json`. Yahoo API (read-only, yfpy; access now needs Yahoo's approval), `python yahoo.py import <snapshot>.txt ["Team"]` (Instinct-style league snapshot or a hand-typed list, `parse_text`; unknown sections are skipped; Refresh imports the newest `*snapshot*.txt` and refetches injuries, stats and box scores (`load(refetch=)`); the top-bar dropdown switches your team via `/api/me`), or `python yahoo.py demo` (fake league from real stats).
- `model.py` also fetches the NBA schedule (`nba_api`, cached a day), ESPN's injury feed (cached 3h, archived per day), ESPN's projections (`espn()`, cached a day, archived per day), this season's box scores (`gamelogs()`, cached 12h, for the player card's recent lines; with this season's stats once a final game is missing from them, at most every 30 min) and live scores (`scores()`, M18: nba_api's live scoreboard + box scores of games under way or final, `data/live.json`, every `LIVE_POLL` 5 min while one is on (`live_on()`), else 30; the last two days kept; a failed box keeps that game's last one) in `load()`, and passes `news()` (ESPN fantasy news: the player list with ids and latest-news times cached 3h in `news_index()`, a player's items refetched only when that time moves) for the card and My team's News panel (`roster_news()`). News never changes a number. `League` built directly (tests) gets none of them, so it stays offline. Freshness (Q9): `SOURCES` lists those caches and their refresh windows. A failed refresh keeps the old copy and is logged in `FAILED` (this season's stats too, which only raise with no copy at all). `freshness()` reads file times against the windows (ok / stale / failed / missing); `load()` keeps the times it built from (`League.loaded`, news read live) and `state()["sources"]` feeds the sidebar.
- `app.py`: stdlib `ThreadingHTTPServer` on 127.0.0.1:8765, routes in `api()`. `live()` (a daemon thread) refetches ESPN's injury feed every `LIVE_MIN` (30) minutes and rebuilds the league on the same snapshot, and on game nights also a minute after each tip-off and every `LIVE_POLL` minutes while one is on (`model.next_poll()`; live scores refetch on their own window, the injury feed stays on `LIVE_MIN`); This week reloads every 5 minutes while a game is on and it's in view; the page pulls `/api/state` again when you come back to the tab after 10+ minutes (`fresh()` clears every view's cache, as Refresh does). Saves the AI GM's chats (`data/chats.json`) and Trades' Offers sent (`data/offers.json`, Q8: each copied offer with what the model predicted and Martin's Accepted / Declined, the data for tuning `accept()`).
- `ai.py`: Claude GM via `client.beta.messages.tool_runner`, tools wrap `League` methods, model `claude-opus-5-5`. Only used when `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set; otherwise `/api/chat` runs `claude -p --safe-mode` (`app.py` `claude_chat`) with the gm SKILL.md as system prompt and only `model.py` allowed, plus `WebSearch` and `WebFetch` when the tab's Web search is on (`WEB` points it at RotoWire's lineups page and the NBA's official injury report) (Model / Effort / Web search are picked on the AI GM tab, validated by `MODELS` / `EFFORTS`). Every chat also passes the app's Waivers / Trades settings as `model.py` flags (`--healthy-fa`, `--no-free`, `--their-free`, `claude_chat`'s `opts`), and Trades' **Ask AI GM** opens the tab with the checked trade in the message box (`S.gmDraft`). Edit SKILL.md and both `/gm` and the in-app chat follow.
- `backtest.py`: replays a real NBA season (game logs cached in `data/gamelogs_*.csv`) in 20 simulated leagues and prints predicted vs actual win rates. It's the evidence behind the matchup knobs.
- `web/index.html`: the whole UI. State in `S`, views are `view*()` functions returning HTML strings, event delegation via `data-act` / `data-view` / `data-punt`.

## Commands
Python lives in `.venv` (Homebrew Python blocks global pip): use `.venv/bin/python` or `source .venv/bin/activate`.
- `python test_model.py`: self-check, synthetic data, no network. Run it after touching `model.py`.
- `python backtest.py`: calibration check (~1 min). Run it after changing projections or odds maths. `python backtest.py replay`: rest-of-season error by expert weight (fits `PROJ_W`). `python backtest.py teammates`: next week's error with teammates' injuries (fits `SHARE` / `USAGE`, ~3 min). `python backtest.py onoff [season]`: the same by `ONOFF_K` (each team's own split, ~5 min). `python backtest.py rates [season]`: the same split for production per minute by stat group (M22, Nov 3: no better on 2022-23 to 2025-26, not shipped; FG% splits don't repeat, ~4 min). `python backtest.py minor [season]`: the same with 15–25 min sitters handing out `SHARE_LOW` of their minutes (`REGULAR` 15) and the `CAP_GIVE` cap (M25, Nov 4: stats worse in 2025-26 and 2024-25, cap no change; both off, ~25 min). `python backtest.py bench`: rest-of-season error by `BENCH_MIN` for players no expert projects, 2022-23 to 2025-26 (M26, ~10 min). `python backtest.py ahead`: from every day of the week, each game left for players who played their team's last game vs `fresh_plays()` (M27, seconds). `python backtest.py gameday`: how often listed players played (fits `GAMEDAY`, seconds). `python backtest.py news`: how often a player who's playing is newly ruled out mid-week, and for how long (fits `HAZARD` / `ONE_GAME`, seconds). `python backtest.py availability`: each Monday's games-played guess vs the real share, 2022-23 to 2025-26 (fits `AVAIL_*`, and `STATUS_AVAIL` from players who sat their team's last game). `python backtest.py playoffs`: each Monday's playoff and title odds vs what happened, 60 leagues (fits `SEASON_SD` / `WEEK_SD`, ~1 min). `python backtest.py recency`: rest-of-season error by minutes shrink and recent games' weight on 2022-23 to 2025-26 (fits `MIN_SHRINK` / `RECENT`, seconds).
- `python app.py --no-browser`: run the app without opening a tab. `#view` and `?punt=FT%25,TOV` deep-link a view and build.
- `python model.py {team|trade|ideas|search|waivers|movers|roster|week|strategy|news|compare|schedule|move|sources} [--punt ...]`: prints JSON (`trade` / `search` take `--no-free` and `--their-free`, and `--healthy-fa` works on any command; `compare --players "A, B"`, Q3; `schedule` = the schedule planner, `League.planner()`, Q4; `move --add A --drop B [--day D]` = the add/drop evaluator, `League.add_drop()`, Q7; `sources` = when each cache was fetched, `freshness()`, Q9; `wants --team T` = what T wants from you, `League.wants()`, Q17; `lineup [--vs T] [--cats C]` = this week's start/sit for the most category wins (or just those categories), `League.start_sit()`, Q18; `note [--text T]` = write a note for Claude Code to `data/notes.md`, or list the open ones, no league loaded). `strategy` (the punt planner) takes ~40s.

## Model rules (don't break these)
- 9-cat advice (trade, ideas, waivers, standings) runs on the matchup model: `week` (per-game line × GAMES_WEEK × avail, missed games half-filled at waiver level) → `cat_probs` → `_score` → `outlook()`. `_score` is the share of categories won (`settings.scoring` "categories", the default and Martin's league) or `match_prob` ("one_win"). Deltas are in win-% points, `unit: "win%"`. Win-% cutoffs (`TIERS`, `VERDICT`, `MOVE`, `GUARD`, `accept()`) are in One Win terms, scaled by `League.s` (`CAT_SCALE` 0.4 in categories). z-scores (`value()`) are only for player tables and candidate selection.
- DISPERSION, SCHED_CV and PCT_NOISE are fitted by `python backtest.py` (2025-26 box scores, 20 simulated leagues, predictions from what was known each Monday, regulars who sat their team's last game out until a noisy return, `RETURN_NOISE`). Re-run it after changing projections (`baseline()`, `prior()`, `ros()`, availability) and keep the calibration tables near the diagonal. GAMES_WEEK and FILL are still guesses (STATUS_AVAIL's SUSP too). After any change, check standings stay realistic: about 37–59% on the real league as categories since N9's availability (41–57% after N5's IL slots; 37–63% before them; 19–80% as One Win back then). The snake-drafted demo is tighter (~47–52%).
- Playoff odds (Q5): `League.playoffs` (in `profile()`) feeds `season_sim()`: the snapshot's `records`, the live week from its totals plus `week_vec`, then each `schedule` week at `vecs` × days / 7, top `settings.playoff_teams` (8) in, a reseeded bracket where the higher seed wins a 4.5–4.5 week (`cat_probs(ties=True)`). Each sim shifts every category's logit by a per-team `SEASON_SD` and a per-matchup `WEEK_SD`; without them a season's category wins spread about 2× too narrowly (2.6× from week 0). Real odds, never punt-conceded. `backtest.py playoffs` shares `season_sim()`, so keep it the one place the maths lives.
- Game-day listings (M16): ESPN's note "listed as probable / questionable / doubtful for Wednesday's game" (`LISTED`) counts that one game at `GAMEDAY` (0.92 / 0.48 / 0.10, measured by `python backtest.py gameday` on the archived feeds vs box scores, 104 player-games, Nov 3) through `League.gameday` and `_plays(k, d, rate)`: `starts(plays=True)` → `week_vec()`, and Start / sit's per-game lines. The game is his team's first on that weekday from the day before ESPN wrote the note (its `date`, UTC). Season-long numbers don't use it. `injuries()` logs every new note to `data/injury_notes.jsonl` (refit there: the day's archive keeps only its last copy).
- Bench fill-ins (M19): This week's expected starts (`starts(plays=True)` → `week_vec()`, and `_week_plan()`'s day values `dv`) come from `_subs()`: each day's players best first, each at his chance to play (`_plays()`), and whoever plays starts while the slots fit him, so a bench player fills in for a starter who sits. Exact: one on everyone's lineup starts whenever he plays; a bench player is decided one teammate at a time (plays / sits) until the rest can't change it, on positions alone (`_fit_n()`), cached in `_lineups`. A locked starter (M18) holds his slot at 1 and nobody fills in for him. In Start / sit a player who'd fit next to the plan's starters with a slot open sits by choice and never fills in. Real league (snapshots Oct 27–Nov 2, each vs the next one's Games played, 15 same-roster team-days with a bench): games −10.3% → −2.7%, PTS −7.5% → −1.9%. `week_vec()` reads `_pw` (`p_week` as an array, fixed at load: tests that edit `p_week` drop it).
- Category odds are calibrated, so cutoffs live in odds terms: `WEAK` (35%) = weak spot / punt hint, and This week's Ahead/Behind calls use the same cutoff.
- Punts concede the category (`_concede` sets your odds to 0) for your side only. Opponents always use real odds.
- `trade()` rejects players you don't own or that come from more than one team (KeyError). Keep that check, since AI tool input comes through it.
- Values are per game, rest of season: `ros()` = minutes (`GP/(GP+MIN_SHRINK)`, 3: fast, roles change) × production per minute (this season's minutes played vs `SHRINK` games of baseline minutes, slow), on this season's line from `recent()`: box scores with each game weighted 0.5^(days ago / `RECENT` 45), GP the real count (fetch()'s line without box scores, or where their games don't equal fetch()'s GP; `gamelogs()` skips games still being played). Regulars and lifts read that same line. `had()` weighs the lifts players had the same way. `SHRINK` was picked replaying 2025-26 (Oct 27); `MIN_SHRINK` and `RECENT` on 2022-23 to 2025-26 (`python backtest.py recency`, Oct 30: −2.0% rest-of-season error, better every season). backtest.py's `projections()`, `replay()` and `mondays()` read `recent()` too: keep them the same maths. `ros()` runs on `prior()`: `baseline()` blended with an expert line at `PROJ_W` (0.6, fitted by `python backtest.py replay` on ESPN's 2025-26 preseason projections). The expert line (`experts()`) is Yahoo's from the snapshot where it has the player, else ESPN's (also when Yahoo's PTS+REB+AST is under half of ESPN's: some Yahoo rows have missed games baked in, Hayes 1.7 pts at 27 min). Yahoo has no minutes: they're ESPN's or ours scaled by output. A rookie's baseline is the fringe line. A player no expert projects (M26, Nov 4) has the fringe line's part of his baseline (`baseline()`'s `PULL` / `PULL_MIN`) at `BENCH_MIN` (12) minutes, his rates kept (`prior()`), and an unprojected rookie in `ros()` starts on the fringe line's rates at `BENCH_MIN` minutes (`python backtest.py bench`: −2.1% rest-of-season error over 2022-23 to 2025-26, every season; hold its leagues' rosters fixed when comparing calibration, since the draft reads the projections). Availability (`availability()`, N9) counts games in the better of the box-score `baseline()`'s two seasons (its `GP`) at `AVAIL_PAST` of a game and this season's in full (out of his team's games in the box scores, as `backtest.py` does), pulled toward `AVAIL_T` by `AVAIL_K` games (rookies: this season toward 85%). Past games barely predict future ones, so availability runs about 61–86% early in the season, not 39–95%. A status tag with no ESPN date cuts it (`STATUS_AVAIL`) only if he sat his team's last game (`League.logs`); an ESPN note matching `REST` (rested, "(rest)", load management) is a one-game rest: status `REST` (not in `OUT`, over Yahoo's O/GTD/DTD, gone once the game is played), its return date still counts the game (Nov 2); `fetch()` counts `SHORT` seasons' games out of 82 (M5). This week (`week_vec()`, `starts(plays=True)`, Start / sit: `_plays(k, d)` with no `rate`) a player who played his team's last game (`League.fresh`) plays his j-th game from today at `fresh_plays()` (M27, Nov 4): M17's late-news chain, (1 − h)(1 − h(1 − `ONE_GAME`))^(j−1), h = `HAZARD` × (1 − rate), since injuries come in spells (`_week_p`; `rate_week` holds his next game's, anyone else's rate); `python backtest.py ahead` checks it from every day of the week (within 2% of each game's share; the flat `WEEK_K` 0.45 it replaced ran 3% low on the next game, 5% high on the 4th). `backtest.py`'s schedule mode does the same, so change them together, and `HAZARD` moves both this and `_reserve()`. Season-long `week`, `vecs` and trades keep `rate`.
- Teammates' injuries (`lifts()`): regulars are players in `League.here` (a game this season or an ESPN listing; before any games, everyone): one who's neither has left the league, not sat out (`had()` took up to 9 min a game off his old teammates for Westbrook and co.), and the market ranks him last. While a regular (`REGULAR`+ min) with an ESPN return date sits, `SHARE` (0.5) of his minutes go to teammates in the rotation by room under `ROOM` (a cap: the rest goes to the bench) and role similarity, plus `USAGE` of the shots, points, assists and turnovers those minutes don't replace. Everyone out at once sits together (M4): a team's players out with a return date form stretches between their returns (`stretches()`), and in each one nobody out takes minutes and the sitters share the room, most minutes first. Each team's own split (M13, `onoff()`, `League.adj`): per receiver, a ridge of his minutes this season on which of its regulars sat (`sat()`, `recent()`'s weights), each sitter's effect pulled toward the rule's minutes as if they were `ONOFF_K` (4) more games, moves the rule's minutes (within `ROOM`, never below zero); minutes only, shots stay on the rule (Fears: +1.3 → +2.7 min while Murray sits). A sitter out since before the season (no games in it) was in the preseason projection, so `priced_in()` cuts his lift by the expert line's part of the receiver's minutes. `ros()` reads this season net of `had()` (the lifts players had in games regulars sat, those out together counted together), so a boost isn't counted twice. `_boost()` adds each stretch's lift for each player's games in it: rest of season in `p`, this week in `p_week` (`week_vec()` reads it). Start / sit (`_week_plan`) instead plays each game on its own day's stretch: the line less the averaged lift (`League.ahead`, or `_boost(week_end)` this week) plus that day's `self.lift` rows, so the games sum to the same week. `League.__init__`, `had()` and `backtest.py`'s `lifted()` must stay the same maths. Lifts only make sense with the injured player sitting; otherwise his minutes count twice. Score `backtest.py teammates` on full weeks: players who miss games that week run 3.6 min under their line, which availability covers.
- "cur"/"Now" = perceived per-game value (`HYPE` shrink). `their_view` uses `perceived()`: that over the games a player plays (`effective()`), floored at waiver level, and an incoming extra player costs the team its weakest players. It's only a note on trades.
- Recent form (Q2): `value(frame="form")` = the last `FORM_DAYS` (14) of `League.logs`, players with `FORM_GP`+ games, z-scored against the rest-of-season pool (`zscores(ref=)`). `trend` arrows and Hot & cold (`movers()`) share one cutoff, `trend_cut(games)` = `TREND` (2.5) × √(`TREND_GP` (7) / games in the window): one standard deviation of a 14-day gap at each game count (M8, Nov 4, `python backtest.py trend`, 2022-23 to 2025-26; the gap doesn't persist into the next 14 days). `movers()` only lists a run with something in it (Nov 1): sell high / buy low need Hot & cold's own price (`League.hot_market`: `_market()` at `HOT_CHASE` 5, leaning on Yahoo's current rank; Martin, Nov 2: **Hot & cold only, never trades**) 15%+ above / below the player's worth to you (his `effective(punt)` rank on the same curve, `edge`), and buy low an `effective(punt)` above the best free agents' (Nov 2: on per-game value Jalen Williams, out till March, was listed), and hot pickups a `_gains()` of `MOVE` (`gain`).
- Trade acceptance runs on the market (N0), never the model's own values: `League.market` = Yahoo Pre/Cur rank blended by `GP/(GP+CHASE)` (20, market only; Hot & cold alone uses `hot_market` at `HOT_CHASE`, don't let it leak into trades) as a geometric mean (so cold and hot starts move a player alike), re-ranked (model's perceived rank when Yahoo has none, `yahoo` False), valued `100 × MARKET^(rank−1)`. Players traded together count as a package (`package()`: the best in full, each next one at `PACKAGE` (0.7) times the one before), so consolidating (a 3-for-1 for a star) costs more than the market sum (Martin, Oct 30). `priced()` carries the plain sums as `sum` for display (Nov 1); acceptance, tiers and ranking read the package; `_priced()` and `search()`'s `stack()` both use it. A deal's market (`priced()`) nets out each side's drops (at their market value) and open spots (each `League.spot`, the waiver level's market value fixed at load, never the pickup's own rank: on a free-move copy the best "free agent" can be your own just-dropped Kessler), the same ones the matchup model makes, so `search()` and `trade()` agree. `TIERS` are (your win %, theirs, market gap range); fair is the market within 15% either way, and hard also passes a gap up to `FAIR_FIT` your way with both gaining (Oct 28: in fair, both ways, those read as unfair). `accept()` / `accept_level()` read the market gap; hard and finesse rank by it, then your gain. A deal never includes a player the side taking him would cut on arrival (`search()` skips it, `trade()` flags it), and a team below `roster_n` active players takes an extra one without a drop (`_room()`, in `_after()` and `search()` alike).
- IL slots (N5): `League.il` = {team: players parked in IL / IL+ / NA slots}, `il_n` slots per team (`settings.il`). A parked player holds no active spot, so he counts through `_return()`, not `week`: no cover for his missed games, and from his return (`back`, share of games left) he takes the spot of his team's weakest active player on `effective()` (the best one back pushes out the weakest; nothing if he wouldn't beat him: he's the one cut). Nov 1: every real team's weakest was below the waiver level the model used before, standings moved −0.9 to +1.4 points. `vec()`, `_gains()` (Waivers, free moves) and `search()` (per deal: who leaves, gets cut, arrives or is picked up) all go through `_return()`; `_contrib()` alone stays additive (it only ranks drops). Trades keep each side's number of *active* players: injured players coming in park in free IL slots (longest out first), sending a parked player frees no active spot, and an injured player coming in is never the drop. `_after()` and `search()` do the same maths; keep them agreeing (test_model checks it). The daily lineup cap is only in This week (`starts()`): season-long it costs 0.1–0.9% of a team's games (measured Oct 27), so `week` leaves it out. Positions too: `starts()` and the planner fill `settings.slots` by position (`lineup()`, via `League._lineup()`), which adds 0.0–0.1% season-long on the real rosters (Oct 28), so trades only flag a starting slot their roster leaves nobody to fill (no C at all costs ~6%).
- Who to cut when your IL players are back (Nov 3, `il_plan()`, My team's Roster panel, `team`'s `il_plan`): every way to keep your active spots' worth of your active players plus them, healthy, on your build; the season numbers keep `_return()`'s cheap rule (the weakest by `effective()`), which matched the best cut on 7 of 9 teams (fatty zhou +0.2 and Umbrella +0.4 otherwise). Ranking cuts by each player's own win % instead was worse (John Ball −1.6): one player's win % on a full roster is too flat to rank by.
- IL players back this week (M21, Nov 3): `_week_roster(team, punt)` = a team's active players plus each parked one with a game left this week from his return (`game_days()`; a game-day listing's chance through `_plays()`), and `{cut: last day}`: he takes an open active spot first (it holds nothing this week), else the cut plays until the day before his first game. Yours: `il_plan()`'s cut (`_il_cut()`), the earliest back taking the one with the fewest games left from then, then the weakest by `effective(punt)` (Ingram Sunday → Black, whose games are done); another team's: `_return()`'s rule. Everything This week builds goes through it: `_matchup()`, `this_week()` (streams, the plan, games, `back`), `_week_plan()` / Start / sit, `add_drop()`, the planner's this-week row and `playoffs()`' live week. A stream or move that drops a player cut for one back is cut in his place (`_handoff()`: the add keeps the cut's last day; `_plan()`'s `ends`, a move's `end`).
- Open spots (Nov 2): a player back from an IL slot takes an open active spot first, then the weakest player's (`_return()`: `_open()` ids are its weakest rows). Until then another team's open spot holds a waiver-level week (`League.level`: the top-5 healthy free agents' average `week`), since its manager can stream it; yours holds nothing, so Waivers tells you to fill it. `vec(keys, il, team)` builds it; everything that builds another team's totals passes `team`: `vecs`, `trade()`'s partner, `wants()`, `_gains()` (an add into an open spot replaces what it held) and `search()` (`now_me` / `now_t`, `returns(..., team, n_open)`, `filled`), so `search()` and `trade()` still agree. This week (M20, Nov 3): `_week_side(team, punt)` = `_week_roster()` plus another team's open spots streamed from tomorrow (while it has `adds()` left) by the `League.level_keys` free agent with the most games in the spot, until a player back from an IL slot takes it (the day before his first game, M21); a real roster member for starts / `_subs()`, his own line. Yours stays empty. `_matchup()`, `this_week()` (`games`, `back.them.stream`) and `playoffs()`' live week use it. Nov 3: no change on the real league (Mumbai's, Toronto's and Shanghai's open spots all go to players back Wednesday, tomorrow). Before it, Shanghai (two IL players dropped, Murray, Sarr and Johnson parked, all back Nov 4) fell from 55.9% (2nd) to 45.1%.
- `effective()` is the only right way to weight value by availability: values are mostly negative (relative to the rostered average), so `value * avail` would rank an injured player above a healthy one.
- Uneven trades always subtract `n * replacement()`. Replacement = top-5 FA average.
- 9-cat `trade()` and `search()` score on top of `after_free()`: your `free_moves()` (greedy add/drop or open-spot add while one clears `MOVE`; never dropping a trade chip, a market rank in the first `CHIP` rounds, `chips()`, M3: Waivers says "sell him instead"; a trade's forced drop is never one while anyone else can go either, M24: `_after()` and `search()`'s `low()` put chips last, `trade()` flags a deal that only fits by cutting one and `search()` skips it) are made first, so a deal gets no credit for a free pickup. The partner is judged on the trade alone (Martin: managers look at the deal, not their waiver options) unless `their_free=True` (Trades toolbar: "Count their waiver moves"). Your pickups are never offered, and the players a deal sends are never dropped (`keep`). A spot a trade opens gets `best_fa()`: yours on your build (`punt`, like `free_moves()`), theirs on real value (`search()`'s `fa_me` / `fa`). You pick first: when both sides open spots, theirs skip your pickups (`_after(skip=)`, `fa[your adds, theirs]`), so one free agent never lands on two rosters. `_check()` drops a name given twice. `free=False` scores on today's rosters. Trades' **Count my waiver moves** (off: `free=False`) and **Count their waiver moves** are independent. Waivers' **Healthy only** sends `healthy_fa`: `app.py` serves state, trades, search and Strategy from `League.healthy_fa()`, a shallow copy with OUT free agents off waivers (rosters unchanged, so the cached team numbers carry over). `add_drop()` (Q7, Waivers' Try a move) is the exception: one add/drop is scored on today's roster like the Waivers table (`_gains`), this week through `_matchup()` / `starts()`.
- Games under way (M18): `League(live=, now=)` (from `load()`; tests pass a fixture). A game dated today locks its NBA teams (`tipped`, `_locked()`) once past its tip-off time or marked under way. `game_days()` drops a locked game, so every rest-of-week number (`starts()`, `week_vec()`, `_week_plan()`, `_timed()`, `_reserve()`, `_week_roster()`, the planner) skips it; `_tipoff` = each team's per-game lineup over its active players with a game today as at tip-off (the finished-day rule of `_so_far()`, so the score doesn't jump when the day ends; a player out with a later return date, or sat in the box score, doesn't count), its locked part; `_days()` puts them first today (they hold their slots, later games fill the rest), `starts()` counts them 0, `_week_plan()` can't move them (`lock`), `_so_far()` adds their live lines.
